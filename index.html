Products

01 · Anomaly Gate

The smoke alarm for your ad spend.

Watches every metric that matters — spend, CTR, CPA, conversion rate, revenue — across every campaign on every platform. When something deviates from its own learned baseline, you get an alert with the root cause attached.

  • Statistical anomaly detection — not threshold rules. Learns each campaign's seasonality.
  • Root-cause attribution — every alert names the campaign, the change, and the likely "why."
  • Slack & email delivery — alerts go to the people who can fix them, with severity tags.
  • Ad-specific & general monitoring — frozen campaigns, spend spikes, tracking breaks, revenue cliffs.

02 · Query Analytics

Find the queries that print money — and the ones that bleed it.

Unifies Google Ads search terms, Search Console queries, and GA4 sessions in one view. Every query is automatically classified by intent so you can see where your budget is going, and where it's earning its keep.

  • Paid + organic unified — one table, every query, with cost, clicks, conversions and revenue.
  • AI intent classification — branded vs generic, transactional vs informational, automatically tagged.
  • Wasted-spend report — queries that took €X with zero conversions, grouped by theme.
  • Negative-keyword suggestions — exportable lists of high-cost, low-intent queries to block.

03 · LP Report

Every landing page, audited honestly.

Combines Core Web Vitals, conversion data, scroll depth, and heuristic UX analysis into a single score per page — plus a prioritised list of what to fix first. Stop guessing which page is the bottleneck.

  • Per-page health score — combining performance, conversion, engagement and heuristic signals.
  • Heuristic UX audit — CTA clarity, form friction, copy readability, mobile tap-target sizing.
  • A/B test recommendations — ranked hypotheses with expected impact and effort estimates.
  • Client-ready PDF export — branded reports with executive summary and action plan.

How it works

Three steps. No spreadsheet archaeology.

Elephant AI connects to your accounts in 3 days, learns what "normal" looks like, then quietly works in the background — only speaking up when there's something you actually need to know.

01

Connect

Connect Google Ads, Meta, GA4, GTM and Search Console with OAuth. No engineering required. Elephant reads — it doesn't change anything in your accounts.

02

Learn

Over the next 3 days the system understands your baselines — your seasonality, your traffic patterns, your campaign-specific norms. Noise vs signal for your account, not a generic industry average.

03

Act

Every alert, insight and recommended action lives in your Elephant dashboard — root cause, affected campaigns, suggested fix, all in one place. Spend ten minutes there a day and you'll know your accounts deeper than any monthly report.
